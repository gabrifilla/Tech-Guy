### 5. GPR-01.4a — Native punch and tornado Conductor coexistence

1. Native Gauntlet punch.
2. Titanic Fists traveling tornado.

A terminal punch may hit enemy A and trigger the native Conductor behavior.

The tornado from that same terminal may subsequently hit another eligible enemy B and trigger its own derived Conductor event.

The desired outcome is **two legitimate Conductor chains when both producers are eligible, suitable secondary targets exist, and shared budgets permit them**.

This is not a guarantee of two chains regardless of gameplay conditions.

Required tests:

- Native Conductor from a direct punch.
- Tornado-derived Conductor after a native whiff.
- Both producers triggering separate valid chains.
- No second chain when budget is exhausted.
- No second chain when no eligible nearby target exists.
- Proper deduplication across colliders, pooled generations and shared lineage.
- No accidental suppression of native Conductor by the tornado's one-emission limit.
- No duplicate damage attribution or resources.

Preserve the existing maximum of one derived Conductor emission per tornado.

Do not increase global Protocol budgets.

If a legitimate second chain is blocked by a confirmed implementation defect, correct it narrowly within the existing Protocol owners.

If this requires changing core balance or fundamental Protocol semantics, stop for approval.

### 8. GPR-01.4d — Mandatory visual evolution

This is a core gameplay-design requirement.

The tornado must visibly reflect the boon combination used by the current committed attack.

#### Visual state A — Pure Wind

- Preserve the current traveling tornado identity.
- Use the established wind vortex as the base visual.
- Do not add elemental layers.

#### Visual state A+B — Electrical Storm

- Add visible electricity integrated into the tornado.
- Lightning may circulate through or around the moving wind vortex.
- The tornado should resemble a small moving thunderstorm.
- Electrical highlights should remain noticeable during travel, even between valid hit events.
- Actual outward chain-lightning damage arcs must appear only when the underlying Conductor event is legitimately authorized and produces its valid gameplay feedback.

#### Visual state A+C — Flaming Tornado

- Add a visibly fiery layer to the moving tornado.
- Use flame-like ribbons, sparks or controlled heat coloration within the vortex.
- Keep the wind silhouette recognizable.
- Fire is an identity layer reflecting Detonation ownership.
- Fire visuals alone must not apply burning, periodic damage, extra collisions or explosions.
- Actual Detonation explosion feedback must correspond to a confirmed valid gameplay event.

#### Visual state A+B+C — Elemental Storm

- Compose all three layers:
  - Wind as the structural base.
  - Fire as one elemental overlay.
  - Lightning as another elemental overlay.
- The combined tornado must visibly contain both fire and electricity while moving.
- Both layers must be individually recognizable.
- Avoid replacing the three-layer identity with a single generic glow or color.
- Avoid visual overload and excessive brightness.
- Preserve enemy telegraph visibility.

**The player should be able to recognize the build composition by looking at the tornado, without checking the inventory or reading damage numbers.**

#### Architecture requirements

Prefer modular visual composition using the existing TitanicFistsVisual instance and pooling/lifecycle contracts.

Do not create four duplicate combat executors or separate full tornado gameplay pipelines.

Layer ownership should be determined from a coherent combat snapshot:

- The projectile must reflect the boons and ranks legitimately available for that committed terminal.
- Picking up Conductor or Detonation after launching a tornado must not retroactively change its effect permissions or appearance.
- A later tornado may use the newly acquired visual state.
- Removing a boon through an authorized state transition should affect subsequent projectiles according to the existing snapshot rules.
- A visual layer must never imply a gameplay effect that the projectile is not permitted to produce.

Separate:

1. Persistent elemental identity while the tornado travels.
2. Actual hit-reactive electrical discharge feedback.
3. Actual confirmed-kill Detonation feedback.

#### Visual implementation constraints

- Reuse existing shaders, materials, particles, LineRenderers and pooling where practical.
- Use the current prototype art direction; do not redesign the character or entire VFX pipeline.
- Preserve tornado speed, radius, distance, lifetime and native damage.
- Avoid allocations or Instantiate/Destroy in repeated hot-path updates where practical.
- Prevent stale visual layers when a pooled tornado is reused.
- Clear all layers after weapon swap, run end, scene change, room clear, disable or context invalidation.
- Completed-room cosmetic tornado behavior must remain safe and must not produce fake damage feedback.

For completed-room cosmetic tornadoes, define and document whether the retained elemental appearance reflects the accepted attack snapshot. Cosmetic fire/electric layers may represent build identity, but damaging chain arcs and explosion feedback must never appear without authorized combat events.

Generate `GPR-01.4d_RELATORIO.md`.
